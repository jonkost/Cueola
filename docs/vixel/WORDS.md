# Vixel's new words

Words Vixel puts on screen that the suite glossary (`docs/v3-glossary.md`) does not have yet, one line each. Jon approves them before they go into the glossary. Each one also has an ⓘ in the app, written in `vixel-mac/Sources/VixelCore/Terms.swift`.

- **output**: the screen Vixel sends its picture to, usually the projector.
- **surface**: a shape on the wall that gets its own picture, like a flat, a screen or the front of a box.
- **corner**: a point you drag to pin one corner of a surface to the real corner on the wall.
- **test pattern**: a picture made for lining things up: a grid, a crosshair, color bars or a solid color.
- **keystone**: a projector setting that squares up a tilted picture; turn it off and let Vixel do the warping.
- **perspective**: how a flat picture looks on a surface seen at an angle; Vixel warps with true perspective.
- **Edit**: the switch that shows every outline, corner and name on the output and lets corners move; off for the show.
- **Grid on All**: the switch that puts the grid on every surface at once, for lining up.
- **Learn Mode**: the View menu switch that shows or hides every ⓘ.
